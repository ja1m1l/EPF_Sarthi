## Service Standards

### PF Final Settlement (Form-19)

PF - Final Withdrawal (Settlement of Form-19): Settlement Time as per Scheme is 20 Days. Settlement Time as per Citizens' Charter is 7 Working Days.

### Settlement Time definitions

Settlement Time as per Citizens' Charter is aspirational and is over and above the time-limits statutorily mandated by the Schemes framed under the EPF & MP Act, 1952.

## Grievance Redressal Mechanism

General time limit for settlement of any grievance shall be 7 working days. In case of non-redressal, the grievance is escalated to the next higher authority.

URL to lodge online grievance: www.epfigms.gov.in (facility also available on UMANG mobile app), www.pgportal.gov.in. 24x7 Call Centre Toll Free No. 1800118005.

## Disaster Provisions

EPFO is now disaster proof and therefore it promises to maintain the service standards even during times of disasters owing to floods, earthquakes, epidemics, etc. in any part of the country.
