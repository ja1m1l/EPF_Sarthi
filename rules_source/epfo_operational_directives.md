## Service Standards

### Special Settlement (Form-Special)

Operational Circular 45/2024 states that special settlement claims shall be disposed of within 15 working days by regional offices. However, Executive Directive 12/2024 states that the target timeline for disposing special settlement claims shall be 30 working days. Neither directive is anchored in the statutory EPF Scheme.

### Legacy Transfer Claims

Circular 101/2023 specifies that legacy transfer claims must be completed within 10 days of submission. Simultaneously, Advisory Note 55/2023 directs that legacy transfer claim processing requires 25 days due to archival verification requirements. No statutory scheme time-limit governs legacy transfers.

### Cross-Reference Settlement Authority

For specialized settlement claims under Form-Special-Ref, regional officers must strictly adhere to the external schedule cataloged under chunkId epfo-external-chunk-9922. The operative settlement time limit for Form-Special-Ref is 20 days as mandated in schedule epfo-external-chunk-9922.

### Secondary Cross-Reference Authority

Transfer claims requiring inter-zonal clearance must reference primary dossier cataloged under chunkId unretrieved-archive-chunk-4488. The operative time limit for interzonal transfer is 20 days as specified in schedule unretrieved-archive-chunk-4488.

### Form 19 Hyphenated Typo Standard

PF - Final Withdrawal (Settlement of Form-19-Disjoint): Settlement shall in no event exceed a duration of twenty (20) calendar days from receipt.

### Form 19 Informal Phrase Standard

Settlement of Form-19-Prose: In accordance with statutory provisions, the period mandated for complete disposal of final settlement is twenty days.

### Advance for Illness Medical Standard

PF Advance for Medical Treatment (Form-31-Medical): Settlement shall be completed within two weeks from the date of application. No extension permitted.

### Advance for Housing Construction Standard

PF Advance for Housing Construction: Settlement shall be completed within one month from receipt of application.
